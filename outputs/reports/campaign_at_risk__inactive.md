### Campaign Strategy: At-Risk / Inactive

* **Target Audience:** Customers categorized as 'At-Risk / Inactive' with High Risk churn risk.
* **Email Subject Line:** We miss you! Here is $25 off your next Electronics purchase

* **Email Body:**  
  Dear Customer,

  We noticed it's been a while since your last visit. We've added a special credit to your account to help you get back to shopping your favorites in Electronics.

  Don't miss out on exploring our curated selections designed to bring you the best value.

  Warm regards,  
  The E-Commerce Customer Care Team

* **Incentive / Promo Code:** $25 Instant Credit + Free Express Shipping on orders over $50
* **Call to Action (CTA):** [Claim My $25 Credit]
